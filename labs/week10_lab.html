1. One orbit, several descriptions

A point rotates counterclockwise on a circle of radius 5 m, once every 20 s, starting at the positive horizontal axis.

A hint to get started

The angle after \(t\) seconds is \((2\pi/20)t\).

Compare your solution after an attempt
\(\omega=\pi/10\) rad/s; \(x=5\cos(\pi t/10),y=5\sin(\pi t/10)\). Polar form is \((5,\pi t/10)\); complex form is \(5(\cos(\pi t/10)+i\sin(\pi t/10))\).

2. Sample a rotating point

Record the motion in Problem 1 every five seconds, beginning at \(t=0\).

A hint to get started

Five seconds is one quarter of the full period.

Compare your solution after an attempt
The positions are \(5,5i,-5,-5i,5\). The recurrence is \(z_{n+1}=iz_n\) with \(z_0=5\); the first return takes four steps.

3. Complex multiplication

In the Motion Synthesizer, set initial radius 3, scale \(\rho=1\), and angle \(\delta=90^\circ\).

A hint to get started

The multiplier has modulus 1 and argument \(90^\circ\).

Compare your solution after an attempt
The positions are \(3,3i,-3,-3i\). Their lengths stay 3 while the direction advances by a quarter-turn.

4. A shrinking spiral

Keep initial radius 3. Use \(\rho=0.8,\delta=45^\circ\), and six steps in the synthesizer.

A hint to get started

The radius is \(3(0.8)^n\), while the total angle is \(45^\circ n\).

Compare your solution after an attempt
After six steps, the radius is 0.786432 and total rotation \(270^\circ\). The point lies on the negative imaginary axis. Shrinking nonzero radii prevent repeated positions.

5. Changing models

Compare \(x^2+y^2=25\) with the time equations in Problem 1.

A hint to get started

Changing the sign of the inside angle reverses circular motion.

Compare your solution after an attempt
The curve equation fixes the path. The parametric equations also fix timing and direction. One reverse motion is \(x=5\cos(\pi t/10),y=-5\sin(\pi t/10)\).

6. Adding displacements

Starting at the origin, take the four successive step vectors \(1,i,i^2,i^3\).

A hint to get started

Add each new step to the current position.

Compare your solution after an attempt
The total is \(1+i-1-i=0\). Positions, including the start, are \(0,1,1+i,i,0\), forming a square.

7. Build an integrated model

Choose a rotating or repeated-motion system. A rotating sign, a wheel, or a point in an animation are possible starting choices.

A hint to get started

Choose simple numbers for radius and period so you can check a few positions.

Compare your solution after an attempt
One example is a radius-2 point with period 8 s: \(\theta=\pi t/4\), \(z(t)=2(\cos(\pi t/4)+i\sin(\pi t/4))\). Sampling every 2 s gives \(z_{n+1}=iz_n,z_0=2\). Other consistent models are welcome.
