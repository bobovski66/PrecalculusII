Circle to Wave

Module 1 · Periodic motion

As a point revolves around the unit circle, its vertical coordinate traces a sine wave. Its horizontal coordinate traces a cosine wave. The graph's horizontal axis records the angle in radians.

Blue is cosine; green is sine. A wave repeats each time the rotating point completes one revolution.
