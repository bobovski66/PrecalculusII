Unit Circle Explorer

Module 1 · From rotation to coordinates

A radian measures how much arc length has been traveled relative to the radius. Change the angle; the moving point's coordinates are cosine and sine.

Angle: rad
Unit-circle point:
Arc traveled: length units
Reference angle: °

Signed arc length counts counterclockwise as positive. A full revolution is 2π radians.

Try these investigations

  1. Set the angle to 180°. What happens to cosine? Explain using the horizontal coordinate.
  2. Compare 30° and 390°. Why are their sine and cosine coordinates equal?
  3. Change the radius. Which outputs change? Why do cosine and sine not change?
  4. Use a negative angle to explore clockwise rotation.
