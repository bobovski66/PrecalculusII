Start at the rightmost point and turn. Compare the physical point on the adjustable circle with the normalized point on the unit circle. Sine and cosine depend on the angle; the physical coordinates also depend on the radius.

Set the rotation

Radians accept entries such as pi/3, -3pi/4, or 1.5. Press Enter or move out of the field to apply.

The slider covers two turns in either direction. Typed entries may go beyond that range. An invalid entry clears the display until corrected.

Try and explain

  1. Compare 45°, 405°, and −315°. Which values match? Which record different journeys?
  2. At 60°, change the radius from 1 to 3. Compare physical coordinates with sine and cosine.
  3. At a negative angle, compare directed arc length with distance traveled.
