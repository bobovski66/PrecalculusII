Sequence and Series Explorer

Module 9 · Patterns and accumulated totals

Choose repeated addition or repeated multiplication. The left graph shows the sequence of individual terms. The right graph shows the running total of those terms. Notice how the two quantities tell different stories.

Axes scale independently to show all calculated terms and sums. Large geometric growth may lead to large values; use the numerical readout to check magnitude.

Investigate

  1. For an arithmetic sequence, keep the difference fixed and change the first term. What happens to the term and sum graphs?
  2. Use a geometric ratio between 0 and 1. Why do the terms shrink while partial sums keep increasing?
  3. Try a ratio greater than 1. Explain the difference between exponential terms and accumulated growth.
  4. Use a negative common difference. Why can some partial sums increase before later decreasing?
  5. Why is a running total a different mathematical object from a single sequence term?
