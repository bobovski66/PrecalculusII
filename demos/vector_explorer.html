Module 6 · Displacements, sums, and dot products

Vectors describe directed changes. Adjust the components of two vectors, then investigate their sum, difference, length, and alignment. The arrows share an origin, but vectors can be translated without changing their meaning.

Blue: u · Green: v · Orange: u + v. The dashed parallelogram illustrates tip-to-tail addition.

Investigate

  1. Set u=(3,4). What is its magnitude, and why?
  2. Set v=(4,−3). What does the dot product tell you about their angle?
  3. Make v=−u. What happens to the sum and to their alignment?
  4. Find two nonzero vectors whose dot product is zero. Explain geometrically.
  5. Explain why the length of u+v need not equal the sum of their lengths.
