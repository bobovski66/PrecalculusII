Curves and Conics Explorer

Module 8 · Shape, parameters, and motion

A curve is a set of points; a parameterization tells us how to travel along it. Choose a family and move the parameter. The graph rescales automatically.

For hyperbolas, the two branches are drawn separately; no line passes through the gap. The parameter slider controls progress along the displayed branches.

Investigate

  1. Choose an ellipse. How do the horizontal and vertical scales change its equation?
  2. Set a=b for the ellipse. What special curve results?
  3. Compare a circle and ellipse. Why are their parameter equations similar?
  4. Choose a parabola. What geometric difference do you notice compared with a closed ellipse?
  5. Choose a hyperbola. What do its two branches have in common?
