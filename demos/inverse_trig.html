Investigate

  1. Compare θ = 30° and θ = 150°. Why does arcsin(sin θ) have the same value?
  2. Try θ = −120°. Which angle does arccos(cos θ) choose?
  3. Why is arcsin(sin θ) not always equal to θ?
  4. At θ = 90°, sine is 1. What is its reciprocal? What is arcsin(1)? Compare the two types of outputs.
