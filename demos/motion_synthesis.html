Discrete Motion Synthesizer

Module 10 · Rotation, scaling, sequences, and curves

Each step multiplies a complex position by a fixed complex factor. The angle of that factor controls rotation, and its modulus controls growth or decay. A geometric sequence in the complex plane becomes a pattern of points.

Blue dots mark individual positions zₙ. The orange arrow reaches the last sample. Dashed segments connect successive samples; they are not a continuous trajectory.

Investigate

  1. Set ρ=1 and δ=90°. How many steps occur before the positions repeat?
  2. Set δ=0 and ρ=0.9. Describe the shape and the sequence of distances.
  3. Choose δ=45° and ρ=0.9. Explain why the result spirals inward.
  4. Choose ρ greater than 1. What changes, and what remains periodic in the angle?
  5. Explain how this single diagram combines the ideas of vectors, complex multiplication, and geometric sequences.
