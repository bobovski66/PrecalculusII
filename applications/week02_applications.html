1. Survey a radio tower

An instrument at ground level measures a \(34^\circ\) elevation angle to the top of a vertical tower, 85 m away horizontally on level ground.

A hint to get started

Tangent compares the opposite height with the adjacent horizontal distance.

Compare your solution after an attempt
\(\tan34^\circ=h/85\), so \(h=85\tan34^\circ\approx57.3\) m. Moving closer increases the angle because the same height is divided by a smaller horizontal distance.

2. A robot's direction

A ground robot moves 12 m east and then 5 m north. We want the direction and length of the straight displacement from its start.

A hint to get started

The angle's opposite side is 5 and its adjacent side is 12.

Compare your solution after an attempt
The displacement has magnitude 13 m, with \(\sin\theta=5/13,\cos\theta=12/13,\tan\theta=5/12\). Its direction is \(\arctan(5/12)\approx22.6^\circ\) north of east. Length alone leaves the direction unspecified.

3. When a slope calculation fails

A program reports a rotating point's direction using \(\tan\theta=\sin\theta/\cos\theta\). Use the Six-Function Explorer.

A hint to get started

Near a vertical direction, the horizontal coordinate is close to zero. Track the sign of that denominator.

Compare your solution after an attempt
Tangent is large positive at \(89^\circ\) and large negative at \(91^\circ\); at \(90^\circ\) it is undefined. Zero tangent describes a horizontal direction. Cotangent is defined there and equals \(0/1=0\).
