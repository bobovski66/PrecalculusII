1. A rider on a wheel

A wheel has radius 12 m, center height 14 m, and period 48 s. A passenger begins at the rightmost point and moves counterclockwise. The height model is \(h(t)=14+12\sin(\pi t/24)\).

A hint to get started

A quarter of the period reaches the top from the original rightmost starting point.

Compare your solution after an attempt
The midline is 14 m, amplitude 12 m, and angular frequency \(\pi/24\) rad/s. Heights range from 2 to 26 m. The first top is at 12 s; \(h(8)=14+6\sqrt3\), and \(h(36)=2\). Starting at the bottom gives \(14-12\cos(\pi t/24)\).

2. A vibrating string

A point on a string has displacement \(y(t)=2\sin(440\pi t)\) millimeters from equilibrium, with \(t\) in seconds.

A hint to get started

Use \(T=2\pi/\omega\) and \(f=1/T\). Angular frequency and frequency use different units.

Compare your solution after an attempt
The amplitude is 2 mm and angular frequency \(440\pi\) rad/s. The period is \(1/220\) s and frequency 220 cycles/s, giving 22 cycles in 0.1 s. Doubling the inside coefficient doubles frequency and halves period; amplitude stays 2 mm.

3. Recover a buoy model

A buoy's measured maximum is 3.5 m and minimum -0.5 m. Consecutive maxima are 10 s apart. At \(t=0\), it crosses the midline upward.

A hint to get started

Average the extremes for the midline and take half their difference for amplitude.

Compare your solution after an attempt
A model is \(h(t)=1.5+2\sin(\pi t/5)\). The amplitude is 2 m, midline 1.5 m, and period 10 s. At 2.5 s it gives 3.5 m; at 7.5 s it gives -0.5 m. Changing wind, overlapping waves, and measurement error are possible sources of differences.
