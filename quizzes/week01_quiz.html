Work each problem before checking it. You can revise an answer after reading the hint. Correct responses remain mastered on this device.

Enter numbers without units in the answer fields. Exact entries such as 5pi/6, -sqrt(2)/2, and 1/2 work. You may also use decimals rounded to three places. Keep units in your written work.

Question 1

Convert 150° to radians.

Hint

Use 180° = π radians.

Question 2

Convert −135° to radians.

Hint

Keep track of the negative sign.

Question 3

Convert 7π/6 radians to degrees.

Hint

Multiply the fraction by 180.

Question 4

Find a positive coterminal angle with −50° between 0° and 360°.

Hint

Add one full revolution.

Question 5

A radius-8 cm circle subtends an angle of π/4 radians. Find the arc length in cm.

Hint

Use s = rθ with θ in radians.

Question 6

At θ = π/3, what is cos θ?

Hint

Think of a 30–60–90 triangle.

Question 7

At θ = 5π/6, what is sin θ?

Hint

The reference angle is π/6; check the quadrant sign.

Question 8

At θ = 5π/4, what is cos θ?

Hint

This is a 45° reference angle in quadrant III.

Question 9

A wheel makes one rotation every 4 seconds. Find its angular speed in radians per second.

Hint

One full rotation is 2π radians.

Question 10

How many radians are in 2.5 counterclockwise revolutions?

Hint

Multiply the revolutions by 2π.
